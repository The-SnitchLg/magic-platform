## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an **Academic Editorial Architecture**—a digital realization of archival rigor, institutional scholarship, and peer-reviewed research curation. Built for academic registries, doctoral candidacies, dissertation committees, and research funding portfolios, the interface delivers deep focus, archival permanence, and elevated dignity.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Editorial Archival Modernism**: Balances historical publishing prestige (monograph serifs, generous typographic hierarchy, warm cream parchment references) with crisp contemporary administrative utility.
- **Tone & Demeanor**: Authoritative, deliberate, contemplative, unhurried. The system avoids gamification, loud micro-interactions, or neon signifiers, favoring silent competence and quiet clarity.
- **Contrast Dynamics**: Employs deep, softened charcoal and warm umber-slate foundations juxtaposed against creamy vellum paper cards and archival linen accents.

## Colors

The color architecture is built on warm organic earth tones and bookbinding pigments rather than cold computational grays.

### Functional Roles
- **Canvas Base (`#1e1c1a`)**: A warm, softened charcoal with umber undertones. Replaces harsh void blacks to reduce visual fatigue during prolonged reading sessions.
- **Surface Layer 1 (`#252220`) & Layer 2 (`#2e2a27`)**: Elevates administrative containers, toolbars, and inspection sidebars into discernible warm-slate planes.
- **Cream Paper Inverted Surfaces (`#faf8f5` / `#f3efe9`)**: Deployed within archival document preview containers, thesis review panes, and prominent callout dossiers to evoke tactile paper leafing.
- **Primary / Terracotta (`#c05c3d`)**: Handled like archival vermilion ink. Used sparingly for interactive focal points, critical research milestones, active chapter tabs, and selective primary actions.
- **Secondary / Sage Forest (`#2d5a3f`)**: The institutional seal of validation. Designates peer approvals, completed milestones, verified grants, and committee clearances.
- **Tertiary / Ochre Scribe (`#c49a45`)**: Denotes works in revision, committee deliberations, and secondary metadata flags.
- **Muted Parchment Ink (`#e7e2da`)**: Standard text color for deep readability across dark fields without spectral glare.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes clear boundaries between discursive, archival content and operational data.

- **Newsreader**: Assigned to structural headings, dissertation abstracts, thesis titles, and publication previews. Renders long-form scholarship with natural academic weight, optical sizing nuances, and editorial authority.
- **Manrope**: Assigned to tabular data, field prompts, administrative controls, navigation items, and timeline metrics. Offers geometric precision and high legibility at micro scales without sterile rigidity.
- **Reading Rhythm**: Long-form body passages (`body-lg`) maintain a comfortable 65–75 character line length with generous 30px line height to facilitate uninterrupted reviewing of research documents.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model adheres to an archival book-margin philosophy with structured columnar balance.

- **Grid Discipline**:
  - **Desktop (>=1200px)**: 12-column grid, 3rem outer margins, 1.5rem gutters. Max-width container capped at 1440px to honor reading proportions.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column grid, 2rem outer margins, 1.25rem gutters.
  - **Mobile (<768px)**: 4-column fluid grid, 1.25rem outer margins, 1rem gutters.
- **Dual-Pane Workspaces**: Core research dashboards employ an asymmetrical layout: a fixed 320px metadata spine on the left and a fluid reading and tracking canvas on the right.
- **Pacing**: Generous vertical rhythm (`space-xl`) between distinct academic chapters or evaluation stages reinforces deliberate pacing over dense SaaS congestion.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally eliminates floating dropshadows, artificial neon glows, and heavy glass diffusion in favor of **archival plane layering** and **tonal containment**.

- **Surface Tiers**:
  - **Base Canvas**: Softened charcoal `#1e1c1a`.
  - **Recessed Insets**: `#181614` for code terminals, transcript logs, and file directory trees.
  - **Raised Plates**: `#252220` for standard institutional cards, table headers, and form groupings.
  - **Floating Toolbars & Modals**: `#2e2a27` bordered by hairline archival rules.
- **Hairline Outlines**: Elements demarcate boundaries via crisp 1px borders rendered in `rgba(231, 226, 218, 0.08)` (subtle chalk) or `rgba(192, 92, 61, 0.25)` when active/selected.
- **Document Elevation**: Paper parchment review sheets (`#faf8f5`) resting inside the dark workspace receive a soft, diffused contour shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(10, 9, 8, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The shape philosophy prioritizes architectural restraint.

- **Level 1 (Soft / 0.25rem)**: Standard buttons, text inputs, table wrappers, institutional badges, and thesis cards utilize subtle 4px corner radii.
- **Strict Rectilinearity**: Archival document previews, split-screen panels, and modal headers maintain flat right-angle structures (`rounded-none` or `4px` maximum), emulating bound folio pages and library index cases rather than playful software widgets.
- **Pills Prohibited**: Stadium/pill geometry is disallowed; all chips and status counters utilize crisp rectangular contours with minimal rounding.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Terracotta `#c05c3d` background with crisp `#faf8f5` text. Hover shifts to `#ab5034`. 4px radius, padded with `0.625rem 1.25rem`. Manrope 13px semi-bold with subtle tracking.
- **Secondary / Archival Outline**: Transparent surface with 1px border `rgba(231, 226, 218, 0.2)` and text `#e7e2da`. Hover shifts background to `#252220`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Text-only `#e7e2da` with an underlined hover state echoing scholarly citations.

### Status Indicators & Chips
- **Approved / Concluded**: Sage forest `#2d5a3f` tinted background (`rgba(45, 90, 63, 0.18)`), bordered by a 1px hairline `rgba(45, 90, 63, 0.45)`, text `#8cb89f`.
- **In Review / Deliberation**: Ochre `#c49a45` tinted background (`rgba(196, 154, 69, 0.15)`), text `#dfbe76`.
- **Draft / Incomplete**: Muted neutral slate (`rgba(231, 226, 218, 0.08)`), text `#a39d94`.

### Lists & Data Tables
- Dense horizontal rules (`1px solid rgba(231, 226, 218, 0.06)`) separating entries.
- Newsreader serif for candidate titles and publication headings; Manrope for dates, ORCID tokens, DOI links, and progress percentages.
- Row hover activates a quiet background wash (`#252220`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Square 16px boxes with 2px corner radii. Unchecked state: 1px border `rgba(231, 226, 218, 0.3)` on `#252220`.
- Checked state: Filled `#2d5a3f` with crisp white validation checkmark, indicating verified sign-off.

### Input Fields
- Dark slate fill (`#252220`), bordered by 1px `rgba(231, 226, 218, 0.12)`.
- Text renders in `#faf8f5`. Focus transition swaps border color cleanly to `#c05c3d` with zero ambient outer glow rings.

### Academic Cards & Folio Containers
- Structural panels resting on `#252220` with hairline borders. Card headers feature small-caps Manrope section trackers over Newsreader display titles.
- Embedded document viewers switch cleanly into parchment paper contrast (`#faf8f5` surface with `#1e1c1a` typography) for proofreading thesis chapters and manuscript submissions.
